# Market-data source and publication audit

Reviewed **2026-11-22 UTC** for the stock-performance update. This is a record of the sources and access actually checked, not a claim that every provider is unavailable or that a legal right follows merely from API access.

## Outcome

**No usable, authorized public price-history snapshot was acquired.** Do not populate the public return chart from search snippets, fixture prices, existing manually reviewed market caps, or an unlicensed CSV. Software readiness and market-data readiness are separate release gates.

The strongest free redistribution candidate is **IEX HIST**, whose exchange terms expressly contemplate distribution with linked attribution. Its current official download page, however, says historical PCAP data is unavailable for direct download. No November 20 file, completed-session print, nine-company coverage, or adjustment history was verified. This is an acquisition blocker, rather than a blanket claim that all exchange data requires payment.

**Databento** is a credible next acquisition route: it advertises historical redistribution after 24 hours for many datasets and an exchange bundle whose venues are free to license for distribution. It requires an account/API key, applicable dataset entitlements and usage credits or funding. No account credential or entitlement was available here. No account was opened, card supplied, subscription bought, or data charge incurred. Exact dataset rights and cost must be confirmed before acquisition.

## IEX attribution and methodology if access becomes available

The exchange requires this text with links:

> Data provided for free by IEX. By accessing or using IEX Historical Data, you agree to the IEX Historical Data Terms of Use.

Link “Data provided for free by IEX” to the exchange and the terms phrase to [the historical terms](https://www.iex.io/legal/hist-data-terms). Retain required notices alongside displays and any permitted exported snapshot.

IEX HIST is **exchange-only**, not the Nasdaq official closing auction or a consolidated US closing tape. A verified complete daily file could support a clearly labelled “last eligible regular-session IEX trade” method if applied consistently and if prints exist for all ranked observations. Do not label that proxy an official consolidated close. Verify trade eligibility, correction/cancellation handling, full-session completeness, timezone/early-close boundaries, symbol identity and split factors. No-print days remain missing; do not carry a stale print into a current ranking. Splits and predecessor tickers require issuer/exchange evidence; the HIST license alone does not supply an adjustment master or current company-wide market cap.

IEX’s historical permission does not automatically extend to the old IEX Cloud/API product, another vendor’s downloaded dataset, or a third-party mirror’s extra terms.
