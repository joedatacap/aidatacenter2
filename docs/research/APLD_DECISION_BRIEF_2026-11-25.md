## Valuation method

Use project cash-flow residuals, then bridge to parent common equity:

1. Model delivered/rent-earning MW by period, contractual rent, operating costs, remaining construction and maintenance spending. Commissioning is not billing. Revenue per MW requires matched periods and capacity.
2. Value each project's cash flows under delivery, cost and discount-rate sensitivities. Allocate project debt service, preferred distributions/redemption and minority participation at that entity. Accounting carrying value is not automatically economic settlement value.
3. Add parent economic interests in other businesses using reconciled ownership; eliminate intercompany double counting. Deduct parent debt/preferred claims and corporate costs. Add only cash actually accessible to the parent.
4. Divide by an instrument-consistent diluted share count. Equity treatment of converts requires removal of the corresponding debt claim; otherwise keep debt and exclude conversion shares. Model warrants and future preferred issuance consistently.
5. Compare to a timestamped price with an explicit margin for error and event risk. A user-selected risk budget divided by entry-to-invalidation distance can size a trade; gaps may exceed the planned stop.

No arbitrary multiple, discount rate or target is inserted. Backlog valuation shortcuts ignore timing, construction obligations and subsidiary claims. New $5.4B base-term revenue spans 18 years; renewal options are not secured base-term revenue. [S6]

**Before a valuation-backed trade:** reconcile current price/capitalization, remaining project spend and obligations, accessible liquidity, Macquarie waterfall, Series G issuance mechanics, ChronoScale diluted ownership, and RFS-to-rent/cash conversion. Until then the useful output is conditional event monitoring, not a fair-value verdict.
