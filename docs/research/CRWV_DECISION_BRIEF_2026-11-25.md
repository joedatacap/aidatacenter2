# CoreWeave: a 30–90 day trading research brief

Reviewed November 25, 2026. Horizon: days to months. This is a conditional research assessment with historical financial inputs, not a live quote, personalized position recommendation or execution instruction.

**Decision:** keep CRWV on a conditional watch until cash collection and financing evidence supports the equity thesis. Revenue delivery is necessary but insufficient: even management's improved operating outlook leaves adjusted operating income below guided interest in Q4. The pivotal question is whether funded delivery improves value per share faster than debt service and additional capital claims consume it.

## Conditional cases and invalidation

**Bull:** service delivery, customer collections and financing remove the market's near-term funding uncertainty. Require FY revenue at least $13B, exit annualized run-rate revenue at least $20B, active power above 1.85GW, and a documented cash-and-funding bridge through delivery. Potential upside mechanism is a lower financing-risk discount; no target multiple or return is asserted. Invalidate if revenue or run rate misses without a credible timing recovery, deliveries slip materially, customer funds are delayed/restricted beyond the funding plan, or issuance/refinancing terms remove the per-share upside. [1–3]

**Base:** the company achieves guidance but construction and financing keep equity economics constrained. Q4 revenue can accelerate while adjusted operating income remains below interest. The share price can move sharply in either direction because meeting guidance is not synonymous with exceeding expectations. Upgrade only when actual receipts, executable project finance and margin progression improve a reconciled per-share model. Downgrade on reduced guidance, financing access or usable liquidity. [1,2]

**Bear:** delayed service or cash receipts coincide with funding needs; more expensive debt or equity issuance transfers value away from existing common holders. The operational trigger is a funding/delivery mismatch, not simply a falling price. Invalidate with verified collections, a funded delivery plan and updated capital structure showing retained common-equity value. Backlog alone does not invalidate this case. [1–3]

These cases have no assigned probabilities; those would imply precision unsupported by the current evidence. A short thesis additionally requires borrow availability/cost and squeeze-risk analysis, which is absent here.

## Financing and dilution audit

DDTL 4 has $8.5B commitment and $4.583B principal outstanding at September 30. It is substantially non-recourse with carve-outs; floating pricing is SOFR + 2.25% or fixed Treasury + 2%. Its approximately 7% reported effective rate is not its coupon/spread. Undrawn capacity is not unrestricted cash and depends on contractual conditions. [2, Note 8]

DDTL 5.5 has $2.6B commitment, $1.555B principal outstanding, Term SOFR + 5.50%, and September 2031 legal maturity. The facility has roughly five-year tenor while initial underlying customer terms average roughly three years, leaving renewal/redeployment exposure. Scheduled final repayment in July 2031 is distinct from legal maturity. Do not apply facility credit ratings to the whole company. [2,5]

The 2033 convertible principal is $4.2B with 2.875% coupon. Initial conversion is 10.2194 shares per $1,000, approximately $97.85/share, subject to adjustments/conditions; settlement can be cash, stock or both. The related capped calls have an initial $199.70 cap and cost $566M. They can reduce specified conversion dilution, not erase debt or all potential dilution. [2, Note 8]

The September 17 ATM program authorizes up to 35M Class A shares. No shares were sold and no collared forwards entered under it through September 30; later usage is unverified. Gross full issuance would increase the dated October 30 basic count by about 6.3%, but proceeds, fees and deployment must also be modeled. It is not mechanically a 6.3% price loss. [2, Note 12;6]

The 10-Q lists 158M instruments excluded from diluted EPS as antidilutive: 100M convertible-note equivalents, 23M options, 31M RSUs/RSAs and 4M warrants. Adding 158M to today's basic shares is not a valid current fully diluted valuation: settlement, vesting, strikes, proceeds, capped calls and debt extinguishment must be treated consistently. [2, Note 13]

The latest retrieved SEC index includes eight November 24 Form 4 filings. Their code-S sales have tax-withholding explanations associated with vesting. Labeling these ordinary filings as discretionary bearish insider selling would be misleading. The old November 24 morning index snapshot need not have been wrong; later filings simply supersede its freshness. [7]

## Valuation that can be defended — and its limits

The November 24 closing-price observation is $42.61 at 16:00 EST, with provider market capitalization $23.69B. This is a dated research observation, not a live executable price. The October 30 issuer cover gives Class A 465,847,732 + Class B 90,277,992 + Class C zero = **556,125,724 basic economic shares**. Multiplying those older shares by that close gives $23.697B, consistent with rounding but not proof that no later shares were issued. [2,8]

A transparent historical claims bridge, in USD billions, is:

`Enterprise value = 0.556125724 × price + 47.505 principal debt + 0.649 finance leases − 4.122 cash = 0.556125724 × price + 44.032`.

At the dated $42.61 observation this is approximately **$67.729B**, not a current verified EV or fair value. Shares, debt and cash have different dates. This convention excludes operating-lease liabilities because rent remains an operating expense; an EBITDAR model must adjust leases consistently. Restricted cash is not deducted. Marketable securities of $62M are excluded conservatively. Other claims, subsequent draws, customer cash and issuance need reconciliation. Vendor EV of $85.69B uses an unverified convention; importing it into this bridge would hide the mismatch. [2,8]

Holding claims and shares constant, each $1B change in enterprise value changes residual common-equity value by approximately $1.80/share. This illustrates leverage sensitivity, not a price target. A $5B debt increase cannot be interpreted as a pure $8.99/share loss without also recognizing the financed assets/cash and their economics.

Do not value a GPU cloud solely on EBITDA or active MW. GPU depreciation/replacement, realized utilization/pricing and contract duration matter. The most defensible next model is contracted cohort cash flows less hardware replacement, financing and dilution, with sensitivity to service delays and renewal economics. Public data here does not yet support a complete cohort model or a fair-value target.

## What remains missing before a trade decision

1. Timestamped executable price/spread and validated volume/volatility/technical entry evidence; no price-based stop or entry is invented.
2. Current share count, ATM/forward use, cash and debt reconciliation; option/convertible treatment under each valuation scenario.
3. Actual prepayment receipts and usable liquidity; full obligations-and-financing schedule without double-counting capex, leases and commitments.
4. Customer and site-level utilization, GPU economics, contractual cancellation/availability remedies, and covenant triggers. Anonymous 30%/24% customers must not be assigned names.
5. Consensus with forecast period and timestamp, and an explicit portfolio risk budget. Fundamental invalidation and price stop are different decisions.
