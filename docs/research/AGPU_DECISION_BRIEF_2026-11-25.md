# AGPU — decision brief, November 25, 2026

**Assessment: an evidence-gated, speculative transition from Access resale to owned Build infrastructure.** The most useful next evidence is customer acceptance, service commencement, incremental gross profit and funding terms. The reported contract portfolio alone does not establish the value accruing to common shareholders. This brief uses public information only; no information from the user's employment or relationships enters the analysis.

The next 30–90 days run approximately December 25, 2026–February 23, 2027. Georgia's revised roadmap extends through Q1 2027: lack of a completion announcement by February 23 would not, by itself, prove that revised window was missed. The **December 17 annual meeting** is a nearer, specifically dated capital-allocation event newly disclosed after the terminal's previous review cutoff.

## Conditional 30–90 day scenarios

**Bull:** Georgia passes customer acceptance and enters service; disclosed billing or recognized revenue confirms monetization. Updated economics under the amended five-year customer contract and a credible funding bridge show incremental gross profit can benefit shareholders. A dated acceptance announcement would improve execution evidence, but it would not alone establish realized margin, cash collection or fair value. Invalidation: failed acceptance, a contract deterioration, unattractive realized unit economics, or funding/issuance that consumes the expected equity upside. [Q][R][G]

**Base:** Access continues and Build remains a staged deployment story while acceptance progresses within the revised Q4 2026/Q1 2027 roadmap. Cash and customer advances support activity, but investors still cannot reconcile a current fully diluted valuation or complete future funding bridge. Another large contract headline does not resolve those gaps. This case changes on verified profitable Build service or a material delivery, customer or funding setback. [Q][R]

**Bear:** acceptance problems, further explicit schedule revision, customer changes or expensive financing reduce the common-equity value of the portfolio. Cash can be consumed fulfilling obligations even with positive reported operating cash flow; token markdowns can reverse reported earnings strength. ATM sales and equity awards may dilute economic participation. These are conditional risks, not findings that issuance or customer failure has occurred. Invalidation: accepted service, demonstrated cash economics and a reconciled financing plan that supports deployment and seller repayment. [Q][P]

No probabilities, stock-price targets, consensus estimates or expected returns are assigned: the necessary current quotation, share reconciliation and forward economics have not been established.

## Cash, debt and dilution audit

At September 30, financing carrying value excluding leases was approximately **$36.889M**: $36.598M Georgia seller financing plus a $291,722 insurance note. Seller financing has **$42.9M contractual face**, no stated coupon and a **17.71% imputed accounting rate**, which is not a market borrowing quote. Monthly installments are $715,000; all remaining amounts become due by September 30, 2027, or earlier upon new cluster-secured financing. The disclosed maturity profile leaves an approximately $34.3M final balloon after intervening installments. September 2027 is beyond this trading horizon, but earlier refinancing can bring repayment into it. [Q]

Recognized operating lease liabilities of **$29.906M** are separate from **$407.4M** of future payments for leases not yet commenced. Approximately **$92.7M** of vendor prepayments are already deployed assets, not additional liquidity. These figures require a time-phased funding model, with overlapping capex and leases reconciled rather than blindly added. The $569.339M contract-liability balance is also presented as remaining performance obligations in the filing: counting both would duplicate the same reported amount. Nine-month continuing operating cash inflow of $465.234M is heavily affected by customer advances; it is neither Q3 cash flow nor recurring free cash flow. [Q]

Historical prefunded exercises are already included in common and must not be added again. The November proxy's beneficial-ownership footnotes include prefunded warrants excluded from beneficial-ownership reporting because of exercise blockers. Those blockers do not erase the economic claim. The proxy's options/award labels also require care: its 1,420,000 unvested plus 2,883 vested awards sum to the 1,422,883 total; adding the aggregate to its components would double count. [Q][P]

Approximately **$83M ATM capacity** remained at September 30. That is potential sale capacity, not cash, a commitment from an investor or proof of subsequent issuance. The proposed new plan reserve is another distinct authorization. Neither belongs in current legal shares before issuance, although both matter to forward per-share scenarios. [Q][P]

A useful valuation worksheet would first establish a timestamped price and same-date economic shares, then independently model Access and Build operating economics, remaining deployment spend, seller repayment, leases, customer obligations and dilution. Customer-funded cash is not automatically excess enterprise-value cash. The $700M full-deployment run-rate expectation should remain a conditional input with its deployment schedule and margins exposed, not an apparent cheap revenue multiple. With these inputs unresolved, a numerical target would manufacture precision.

## New-position and existing-position workflows

For a **new position**, the research supports an acceptance/margin/funding watchlist thesis. Before sizing, obtain a current executable quote, spread and trading liquidity; specify the evidence required to initiate, the maximum portfolio loss permitted and the response to adverse disclosure. An exploratory position before evidence arrives is an explicit execution-and-financing speculation. This brief does not establish an entry price or favorable risk/reward at today's quote.

For an **existing position**, restate the thesis from today's evidence rather than the entry price. If the thesis depends on a profitable current Build business, Q3 does not support it. If it depends on future acceptance, track the staged evidence and preserve the original Q3 miss and revised roadmap in the record. Review position exposure before the December vote and any acceptance or financing announcement. A tax lot, position size or cost basis was not supplied, so no personalized trim quantity or stop level is invented.

For both workflows, useful invalidations are explicit failed acceptance, a new adverse timetable, customer/contract deterioration, financing terms that break the per-share model or demonstrated margins below the investment thesis. A price decline alone does not prove an operational failure; silence before the revised window expires does not prove lateness.
