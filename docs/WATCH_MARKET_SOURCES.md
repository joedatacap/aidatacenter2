## IEX attribution and methodology if access becomes available

The exchange requires this text with links:

> Data provided for free by IEX. By accessing or using IEX Historical Data, you agree to the IEX Historical Data Terms of Use.

Link “Data provided for free by IEX” to the exchange and the terms phrase to [the historical terms](https://www.iex.io/legal/hist-data-terms). Retain required notices alongside displays and any permitted exported snapshot.

IEX HIST is **exchange-only**, not the Nasdaq official closing auction or a consolidated US closing tape. A verified complete daily file could support a clearly labelled “last eligible regular-session IEX trade” method if applied consistently and if prints exist for all ranked observations. Do not label that proxy an official consolidated close. Verify trade eligibility, correction/cancellation handling, full-session completeness, timezone/early-close boundaries, symbol identity and split factors. No-print days remain missing; do not carry a stale print into a current ranking. Splits and predecessor tickers require issuer/exchange evidence; the HIST license alone does not supply an adjustment master or current company-wide market cap.

IEX’s historical permission does not automatically extend to the old IEX Cloud/API product, another vendor’s downloaded dataset, or a third-party mirror’s extra terms.
