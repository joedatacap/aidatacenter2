NBIS · strongest fact and objection

The base requires $11.36B of net new equity through November 27, 2027, even with customer advances and project loans. That means 151.56M financing shares, plus a separate 15.23M reserve for share awards.

Against the call: Management expects advances and asset-backed financing to cover 75–85% of 2027 capex. If spending is 20% lower while revenue and financing remain unchanged, modeled value rises to $132.32; preserving revenue with less spending is the optimistic assumption.

Observable rating threshold: Upgrade only when a refreshed model offers at least 25% upside and no more than 25% dilution, supported by documented funding for at least 75% of planned capex and remaining New Jersey delivery by Q2 2027. Downgrade if funding coverage falls below 75% without replacement, or further delivery delays impair customer economics.

CRWV · strongest fact and objection

Collect the stated customer deposits, finance installed equipment with actually closed project facilities, and retain attractive renewal pricing. The base requires $7.74 billion net hypothetical equity and 206.19 million financing shares, far beyond the 35 million-share September 30 ATM capacity.

Against the call: Management says project loans, prepayments and operating cash can cover the significant majority of capital needs; new contracts and paid-off GPUs may make this model too conservative. Conversely even 6.8 x EBITDA assumes 60% normalized unlevered cash conversion after maintenance replacement and tax, which is not established by disclosures.

Observable rating threshold: A Buy case needs confirmed collection of the approximately $9 billion deposits expected by roughly February 2027, sufficient closed project financing to replace the hypothetical debt draws, and a credible replacement-capex burden. Failure to obtain that funding or cash conversion invalidates the base rather than creating an automatic equity rescue.

Next three deep research priorities

  1. APLD · How much of TopCo 2 cash and lease cash reaches APLD common after cost to complete, project debt service, Macquarie preferred/minority claims and actual Series G issuance?

    Largest immediate distinction between consolidated liquidity and common-equity value among the seven not receiving the NBIS/CRWV full deep dives. Build a dated project-to-parent cash waterfall before treating a low headline multiple or tactical bounce objective as valuation evidence.

  2. AGPU · After reconciling common shares and prefunded exercises at one date, do accepted Georgia billings and cash gross margins cover customer obligations, seller/debt payments and replacement costs per economic share?

    Both numerator and denominator can materially change apparent upside: dated legal shares understate economic claims, while energized GPUs and lifetime contract amounts do not prove accepted revenue or surplus cash.

  3. SHAZ · Which quarter-specific cash capex payments behind the $5.35B disclosed commitments are covered by actual customer receipts and drawn facilities, and what equity or recourse funding remains before service acceptance?

    The explicit RPO timetable enables a disciplined cash bridge, but the scale and conditions of commitments, customer-linked facilities and guarantees can change common-share value more than additional TCV announcements.
