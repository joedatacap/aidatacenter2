# Neocloud Watch data audit

Audit performed November 16 evening Eastern / November 17 UTC, 2026. This is a bounded source and logic audit, not independent certification of every historical fact.

## Findings and corrections

- IREN Q1 FY2027 ended September 30, 2026 and was released November 5. Applied Digital Q1 FY2027 ended August 31 and was released October 7. These are recent fiscal quarters, not calendar Q1. Overview and earnings tables now lead with quarter-end dates and retain fiscal labels and publication dates.
- SharonAI Q3 was published November 16 after the original research cutoff. Added the quarter from the SEC 10-Q and issuer release. Revenue $9,930,669; operating loss $37,461,270; issuer adjusted EBITDA loss $25,769,000; quarter operating cash outflow $66,090,364. Updated cash, borrowing carrying values, excluded balances, deployment evidence and outlook together. Removed the completed release from upcoming events.
- SharonAI September cash is $1,341,257,049. Borrowings excluding leases are $1,007,776,066: $1,007,749,910 noncurrent convertible notes + $20,059 current convertible notes + $6,097 note payable. Restricted cash $158,687,424 and noncurrent certificates $162,752,568 remain separate. Carrying amounts do not equal contractual principal.
- November 17 update: Axe Q3 revenue $7,243,043; operating income $6,825,897; net income $11,503,607; adjusted EBITDA loss $2,275,000. Profit includes substantial asset gains. September cash $147,710,376; financing obligations $36,889,299 including discounted deferred acquisition consideration. Customer prepayments $569,338,973 and leases $29,906,360 are separate. Georgia acceptance remains underway. Nine-month cash flows are not quarterly figures. Historical consolidated periods are not automatically comparable with Q3 continuing operations.
- Nine companies have earnings coverage. Ten contextual peers do not. The 19-company universe must not be presented as 19 fully researched earnings histories.

## What was checked

Inventory of every company's statement, earnings and market date; all embedded source references; quarter ordering and period lengths; latest focus-company financial exhibits; selected cash-flow columns and derivations; distinction between fiscal and calendar periods; cash restrictions; completed report events; operating versus planned capacity; and displayed coverage boundaries. Latest revenue and earnings figures were checked against the loaded primary exhibits. Numeric occurrence checks were used only as a screening aid, never as proof of matching period, basis or meaning.

## Remaining limits

- This pass does not independently re-underwrite all historical quarters, every instrument, or all source records. Historical financial statements and debt terms remain dated observations, not assertions of today's balances.
- Some historical records are later comparative disclosures. Their source publication date is not necessarily their original earnings announcement date. Do not use them to calculate historical price reactions.
- Market capitalization remains a dated provider snapshot (mainly November 13; contextual peers November 12). It is not a live quote. Nebius, SharonAI and Axe capitalization reconciliation remains incomplete; qualified/withheld ratios must remain so.
- Consensus and T+1/T+5 returns are not populated. The site cannot claim earnings beats/misses against Wall Street expectations.
- Buildout is selected reported evidence, not a complete inventory or proof that all announced capacity is live. Planned, contracted, energized, accepted and billing are different stages. Individual and portfolio contracts may overlap.
- Adjusted EBITDA definitions differ across issuers. Hut 8 includes substantial non-cloud activity and changed its adjustment policy; company-wide operating margin is not AI-cloud margin. Applied Digital revenue includes tenant fit-out work and consolidated ChronoScale, while its adjusted measures exclude ChronoScale.
- SharonAI cash capex is the $8,507,319 purchases line only. $293,055,438 equipment advances are separately disclosed and excluded from that line; it is not a measure of all infrastructure cash spending.
- No recurring source-refresh job is running. A deploy time is not a fresh verification date for every field.
