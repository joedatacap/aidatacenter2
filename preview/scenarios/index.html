What changes in each scenario?

Each company starts with the same synthetic operating inputs and a $2B illustrative equity denominator. HUT and NBIS have committed undrawn funding in this fixture; other labels have uncommitted funding. CRWV has $500M unhedged floating principal; the others have $200M after a $300M assumed hedge. These differences teach the model; they do not describe the companies.

Rate shock changes immediate annualized interest on unhedged floating debt. The funding engine separately includes an explicitly assumed future refinancing effect under Tight credit. Fixed coupons do not reprice.

03 / BUILT TO SHOW ITS WORK

The question behind the numbers.

01

Who needs capital?

Compare uses with usable sources over 12 or 18 months. An announced facility is not automatically cash available to spend.

02

What actually reprices?

Separate fixed coupons, floating-rate exposure and future refinancing. Missing market yields stay unavailable.

03

What can we trust?

Keep assumptions apart from reported evidence. No composite safety score, invented quotes or company-wide guarantees.
