# Decision workspace release plan

Owner request: substantial upgrade for new and existing 30–90 day positions in CRWV, APLD and AGPU. Existing Watch homepage remains intact. Public research only; user notes remain private to the browser.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each company has a sourced decision brief: central question, bull/base/bear, current evidence, counterarguments, dated checkpoints, invalidation, and unresolved valuation inputs.
2. Refresh primary filings and issuer releases through November 25, 2026; retain old commitments and separate announced/commissioned/accepted/billing/revenue stages.
3. Editable scenario lab displays CoreWeave’s enterprise-to-equity bridge and reverse valuation, plus APLD/AGPU common-equity residual cash-flow models and dilution sensitivity. Missing inputs block outputs. No fabricated consensus, live quotes, probabilities or target prices.
4. Decision journal supports both new and existing holdings, explicit user decisions, long-position risk arithmetic, dated snapshots, JSON backup/import and printable research. No brokerage execution or synchronized team storage claimed.
5. Disclosure pipeline finds new SEC/IR items and stages a review queue. Retrieval failures remain visible. No auto-promotion of extracted financial claims.
6. Meaningful numerical, validation, desktop/mobile and existing Watch regressions pass. Publish to the existing Site, with release provenance in GitHub.

## Work streams

- CoreWeave primary evidence, capital structure and case analysis.
- Applied Digital delivery and financing refresh.
- Axe public disclosures, share denominator and acceptance milestones.
- Disclosure discovery with explicit review boundary.
- Pure scenario and position arithmetic with edge-case tests.
- Integrated UI, evidence validation, review and publication.

## Product boundary

Research verdicts are conditional interpretations with sources, not mechanical trading signals. A useful output can be 'wait for evidence' or 'valuation not yet defensible'. Prices must be supplied by the user with date/source, or a licensed source. Existing official TradingView widget remains intact. Historical basic shares are not represented as current fully diluted shares. No paywalled service purchases.
