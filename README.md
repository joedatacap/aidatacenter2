# NeoCloud Debt Intelligence

**Crusoe Recursive Agents Hackathon | October 17, 2026**

**Which AI infrastructure companies survive higher rates?**

An autonomous research system that investigates publicly listed AI cloud companies' debt structures, links financing to customer deals where supported by actual disclosures, and compares exposure to rising rates and refinancing risk.

## MVP

Enter one or two tickers (initially **CRWV**, **NBIS**, **IREN**) and investigate:

- Debt financing instruments, principal outstanding, coupon or floating spread, maturity, hedges and recourse.
- AI customer contracts and **evidence-verified** financing relationships.
- Dated stock-price observations where reliable market data is available.
- Rate-shock scenarios that distinguish near-term unhedged floating expense from future refinancing assumptions.
- A genuine Crusoe-powered autonomous agent team that plans, delegates, audits its findings and launches follow-up investigations.

Every number should carry source evidence, currency, as-of date and unknown-data warnings. Do not conflate a fixed bond's coupon with its current traded yield.
