## CRWV · CoreWeave

**Hold / no new fundamental Buy until financing and replacement economics are verified** · Low on fair value; moderate on identified financing and delivery risks. Six explicit cases are decision sensitivities, not consensus estimates. conviction · 12 months

Contracted revenue supports a multi-year operating ramp, but a cash-intensive delivery model can increase debt faster than cash generation. The question is not whether backlog is large; it is how much sustainable unlevered cash survives hardware replacement and then belongs to common shareholders.

- Around2027-02-10 · Review three-month customer-cash expectation; analyst window, not contractual deadline

### Risks

- Shorter customer terms than project finance tenor create renewal/redeployment exposure.
- Annual recognized revenue may lag capacity deployment and funding; take-or-pay is conditional on service obligations.
- Dilution, refinancing terms and GPU replacement costs can dominate shareholder returns despite EBITDA growth.

### Thesis breaks

- Bear concern weakens if verified collections plus executable financing cover delivery without disproportionate dilution and replacement economics support sustainable cash conversion.
- Positive long-term thesis fails if recognition delays persist, financing cannot close, or annual cash capex fails to moderate without harming contracted delivery.

### Evidence needed

- Site/cohort replacement and utilization data: obtain issuer deployment/renewal economics and capex split in earnings disclosures.
- Current fully diluted capitalization and cash restrictions: reconcile next10-Q/8-K, ATM usage, conversion settlements and project covenants.
