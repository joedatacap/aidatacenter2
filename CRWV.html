      What remains an assumption or an evidence gap

      • No verified closed material financing after September 30 is added. SEC daily master indexes through November 25 show only Form 144/4 since November 17; November 26 was a US holiday. The IR index is partly rendered, so this is a bounded check rather than a guarantee of exhaustive issuer news.
      • Nine billion of prospective prepayments/deposits is a November 10 management expectation through roughly February 10,2027, not a confirmed subsequent cash balance. Collection, restriction, refund and recognition schedules are not publicly reconciled.
      • Quarterly 2027 principal repayment and capex timing are analyst allocations of annual totals. DDTL5.5 cash sweep and covenant-related accelerations can make actual payments earlier/higher than the smooth modeled schedule.
      • Future project borrowing well beyond disclosed undrawn capacity is required in every case. New-debt assumptions include refinancing and are not committed facilities. Failed loans or equity issuance invalidates the scenario; the calculator cannot guarantee funding.
      • The 35 million-share September 30 ATM capacity cannot fund the modeled downside/base equity gaps by itself. Any excess modeled issuance needs other registered offerings/authorizations and market demand; availability after September 30 is not independently confirmed.
      • No automatic conversion of out-of-money notes. Potential EPS-excluded options, warrants and unvested awards are not automatically added to basic shares; additional-share reserves are analyst estimates, not a fully reconciled vested/treasury-stock denominator.
      • Maintenance GPU replacement capex, recontracting retention and cash tax/capitalized-interest reconciliation remain insufficient for a defensible unconditional target. EBITDA cash-conversion anchors are illustrative and challenged explicitly; no implied peer multiple or fabricated consensus.
      • Enterprise valuation uses rent-expensed issuer-adjusted EBITDA. Operating lease liabilities are not also deducted as EV debt; operating rent is already in the cash operating margin. Finance leases are separate claims with cash payments in other uses. No NCI/preferred balance is reported; project cash leakage/claims could still differ.

      • Quarter/stub receipt and spending timing are assumptions; intraperiod shortfalls may exceed period-end gaps.
