## Financial reconciliations

WhiteFiber debt uses the balance-sheet carrying lines, in USD thousands: 11,873 current third-party debt + 29,989 related-party debt + 330,088 convertible notes + 30,683 noncurrent third-party debt = 402,633. The related-party balance includes accreted MOIC, so the separate principal table is not substituted. The $300M subsequent NC-1 facility is a commitment subject to funding conditions, not September drawn debt. Current 2031 and 2032 coupons remain instrument references; selected rates do not become a company average. The floating Iceland formula has no all-in rate without a dated SOFR fixing.

SharonAI debt uses the June balance-sheet values in USD: 1,006,515,000 noncurrent convertible notes + 20,059 current convertible notes + 6,025 note payable = 1,006,541,084. The convertible carrying value includes fair-value measurement and must not be described as outstanding contractual principal. Finance leases of $4,816,430 and restricted certificates of deposit of $12,748,105 are separate. The September 28 subsidiary $356M facility is a later commitment. Its initial Facility A cash rate of 7.25% steps to 9.95%; Facility B is 9.95%. Parent recourse is limited/releasable under stated conditions, not assumed already released. No drawn amount is inferred.

Axe's reported $413,611 note payable is insurance-premium financing, with a 6.1% contractual rate and installments through April 2027. June contract liabilities of $60,754,672 are customer prepayments, not conventional borrowing or current revenue. Digital assets and related receivables are not added to cash. The subsequent September Georgia acquisition retired approximately $87.8M of SPV asset-backed financing and left $42.9M of deferred purchase consideration; those later observations are preserved separately, without making a pro-forma June borrowing total. Full GPU hardware ownership does not establish an exact customer acceptance date or operating IT MW.

AGPU capitalization remains blocked pending basic-share and derivative reconciliation. The June filing reports 11,384,940 closing common shares and explains that 9,972,424 prefunded warrants enter basic EPS computation. Weighted-average EPS shares are not a closing outstanding-share count. The 1-for-15 September 2025 reverse split is retrospectively reflected in filing amounts; convertible preferred, warrants, options and later issuance are still material. No independently reconciled market capitalization or diluted scenario is inferred.

## Quarter and metric treatment

WhiteFiber's Q3 revenue is $24.017M, GAAP operating loss is $12.403M, and issuer-adjusted EBITDA is negative $24,000 rather than artificial zero. The quarter includes termination-fee revenue and lease-accounting changes; these caveats remain attached. Different filing precision is retained, so consecutive quarterly rounded amounts need not sum exactly to a more precise annual or YTD value.

SharonAI Q1 uses the issuer presentation in rounded USD thousands, including $294,000 revenue and negative $2.209M adjusted EBITDA. Its Q2 release reports adjusted EBITDA rounded to $0.6M. Later Q2 YTD figures are not silently used to overwrite Q1 presentation precision. Quarter-only operating cash flow and cash capex remain explicit unresearched fields where a compatible YTD bridge was not reviewed. Cash paid for equipment, supplier deposits, noncash equipment additions and leases must not be collapsed. No YTD cash-flow amount has been displayed as a quarter.

## Delivery and catalysts

WhiteFiber's full NC-1 billing target at end-August appears in the Q2 filing. The November release reports all 40 MW live, full billing begun, and an acknowledged commissioning delay. The outcome establishes a missed original timeframe followed by reported delivery; the exact final acceptance day is not independently established, and the entire 40 MW is not backdated to September 30.

SharonAI's August 20 issuer release establishes initial customer acceptance under a roughly $950M five-year agreement and escrow-security release. Its 212 MW secured / 120 MW contracted figures are not accepted operating totals. Precise originally promised acceptance deadlines are unavailable, so schedule adherence remains insufficient evidence even though the delivery itself is reported.

Axe's May and August releases target Q3 go-live for the 2,304 B300 cluster. The October SPV purchase establishes ownership and a revised five-year customer term; it does not explicitly establish the exact acceptance date or recognized Q3 revenue. An expired target without a verified outcome is **insufficient evidence**, not automatic non-delivery. The November 13 issuer correction changes the Q3 call date from November 16 to November 17; copied calendars must use the correction.
