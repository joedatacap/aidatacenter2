## Audit of the supplied credit graphic

- **Keep the distinction between the borrower and the customer.** Lambda's closed issue and IREN's different facilities demonstrate the spread in actual financing terms. An IG end customer matters only to the extent its contract, guarantee or termination payment supports the specific debt. Check who signs, what the lender can enforce, when protection starts and who bears the loss before acceptance.
- **CoreWeave's $4.5B Dalton facility is a market report.** The size, proposed pricing, initial indications and anticipated rating can inform a scenario. The signed financing ledger requires a closing, and cash availability needs evidence of eligible draws. The $4.5B cannot be added automatically to modeled borrowing.
- **The parent-bond stress has dated observations.** The 9.75% 2031 was reported at 75.50 with calculated 17.43% YTW. The 9% 2031 showed 17.18% calculated YTW on Friday and an earlier trade above 18%. Exact trade identifiers, times, volume, trade yield and calculated YTW are in the [CoreWeave note](credit/CRWV_NBIS.md). Public secondary TRACE reproductions supplied the recent values. Direct FINRA-certified prints were unavailable for this review.
- **CDS and private loan prices need attribution.** Bloomberg cited intraday ICE pricing on November 25. A greater-than-70% implied default probability is a risk-neutral estimate sensitive to recovery and pricing assumptions. No Friday closing CDS series was verified. Near-par DDTL trading is attributed market commentary without specific independent prints.
- **Avoid blanket numerical assumptions.** The proposed 60–80% advance rate, universal escrow structure, full elimination of GPU obsolescence risk, default-rate extrapolation, and uniform 7% funding rate have insufficient support across the cohort. Unnamed offtakers can be rated. Borrowing via a parent, project company, GPU pool or real estate issuer has different consequences. Convert coupons and yields also include the value of the equity option.
- **Follow-up data.** Verify H100/B200 settled rental rates with source, region, term, volume, availability and GPU vintage before using them in a recovery sensitivity. An equipment rental transaction can inform fleet economics but cannot establish a bond or loan price. Weekly equity/HYG performance and the supplied comparative move were outside the credit-cost calculation.

## Decision rule

Use an executed instrument-level rate for existing debt. Keep issuance coupon, original issue discount, dated secondary yield, fees, and accounting effective rate in their own fields. Use a low-cost structured scenario for new financing only when the specific contract, eligible collateral, draw conditions and recourse justify it. Otherwise carry a range and a delayed-financing case. Flag unsupported low-cost assumptions without assigning a distressed parent yield to another borrower.
