### Axe Compute (AGPU)

**Above-7% risk flag supported by an issuer estimate.** In its November 17 filing Axe used **17.71%** as its estimated borrowing rate for similar one-year financing secured by the Georgia cluster. This was an unobservable accounting input. The actual seller obligation has **no stated interest**. Neither a lender quote nor a traded unsecured yield was verified. Axe reported that it had no rated debt. [Q3 10-Q, Note 1 and critical accounting estimates](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1446159/000117184326007507/agpu20260930_10q.htm)
