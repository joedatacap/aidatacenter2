# Applied Digital | What reaches common shareholders?

**Research decision, November 27, 2026:** Retain **Under review** for the 12-month fundamental call. The Oracle-backed Harwood project supports secured borrowing near the credit framework's reference rate. Its stabilized midpoint leaves about **$39.85 million a year after illustrative initial debt service**, before reserve funding, reinvestment and preferred claims. Public evidence does not yet establish a positive ordinary cash distribution to APLD common shareholders from this project. The separate 30–90-day tactical Hold existing / Wait new remains a distinct historical call. ## Polaris Forge 2, Harwood | 200 MW

March's closing 8-K identifies Oracle as the tenant. As of November 24, the first 25 MW hall had reached ready-for-service and 75 MW had completed base-build commissioning. The figures describe overlapping milestones. Actual project cash collections, the full 200 MW final lease commencement and project distributions were unverified at cutoff. The table combines lifetime average rent and stabilized margins with opening debt service to show the size of the cushion. Actual annual cash rent can differ materially, including during ramp and escalation. Principal begins accruing after final commencement, interest declines as principal amortizes, and maturity can require refinancing. The midpoint NOI / illustrative debt service is **1.17×**. At the midpoint margin, rent 15% below the levelized amount leaves about **negative $1.61m** after the same debt service. This sensitivity assumes the debt is amortizing and shows the impact of lower rent in that state. It does not simulate a pre-commencement delay. ### Why the last row is open

1. **Project account.** Trustee-controlled accounts cover operations, debt service and reserves. The ordinary retained-excess-cash and 30%-of-trailing-NOI distribution baskets require final commencement. Other baskets separately permit certain payments such as qualifying taxes and corporate services. An available basket creates permission, not proof of a transfer. Actual lease receipts, reserve top-ups, capex and distributions by project are outstanding. [Indenture, §§1.01, 4.05(b), 4.23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1144879/000149315226009538/ex4-1.htm)
2. **TopCo 2.** Available excess cash goes first toward specified losses, unpaid preferred dividends and Macquarie's liquidation preference, then pro rata to common units. The original four equity closings totaled $1.825bn. Preferred accrual starts at 12.75%, compounds semiannually and has contractual step-ups. The minimum 1.80× invested-capital preference includes the common-unit value and has exceptions and date-specific redemption terms. It is a portfolio-level claim. Actual August-quarter dividends paid in kind were $60.1m. Avoid charging that entire amount to Harwood, or subtracting PIK twice at redemption. [10-K, note 16, pp. 117–118](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1144879/000114487926000048/apld-20260531.htm) · [10-Q, notes 12 and 16](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1144879/000114487926000059/apld-20260831.htm)
3. **Listed parent.** Cash arriving at APLD still competes with corporate interest, overhead, taxes and new construction. The November 24 parent Series G amendment lifted total commitment to $2.5bn with $1.035bn still available. In fiscal Q1, $275m of gross Series G proceeds and conversions of 154,500 preferred units into 4.5m common shares were reported; the 128,750 units left at quarter end converted into another 5.2m shares after August. Apply future proceeds and associated dilution once, at dated terms. [November 24 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1144879/000149315226054002/form8-k.htm) · [10-Q, notes 16 and 21](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1144879/000114487926000059/apld-20260831.htm)
